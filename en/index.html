FAQ

Good to know.

When can I book?

The studio is available from 1 July to 31 August 2027. Send us your dates via WhatsApp, by email or with the form, and we will confirm availability and your rate.

How do I get 10% off?

Book 7 nights or more directly with us rather than through a platform, and 10% comes off the nightly rate.

Is parking included?

Yes, a private parking space is included – a real plus on the Riviera in summer.

How does check-in work?

Check-in is self-service: you receive the instructions and access codes before your stay and can arrive whenever suits you.

Is the pool open?

The residence pool is open from May to October, so throughout your summer stay.

Which languages do you speak?

French, English, German, Spanish, Italian and Croatian.
